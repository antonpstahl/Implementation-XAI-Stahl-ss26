=== LIMITATIONS PARAGRAPH (temporal split) for the thesis ===

The model evaluation is based on a day-level random 70/30 split: the 731
calendar days are randomised as whole units (511 train / 220 test,
no day in both), so every hour of a given day falls on the same side. This closes the
strongest leakage channel in hourly data, where neighbouring hours of the same day would
otherwise land in different splits. What it does not remove is between-day
autocorrelation: adjacent days resemble each other in weather and season, so the measured
generalisation may still be mildly optimistic.

As a robustness check a temporal split was run: the first 70 percent of the data in
chronological order (2011-01-01 to 2012-05-27) served as the training set,
the remaining 30 percent (2012-05-27 to 2012-12-31) as the test set. The
results are worse compared to the day-level split (dRMSE XGBoost:
+39.64, EBM: +24.38; dR2 XGBoost: -0.0844,
EBM: -0.0357). The metric divergence is moderate. It should not be read as
the size of the residual leakage in the day-level split: a chronological split also
removes the second half of 2012 from training and with it the contrast the `yr` feature
is learned from, and the two effects are not separable from this comparison alone.
For the XAI demonstration of this work the absolute model quality is secondary; all
pipelines use the same models and the same split, so the relative ranking of the
explanation pipelines is unaffected by this limitation.
