=== LIMITATIONS PARAGRAPH (temporal split) for the thesis ===

The model evaluation is based on a random 70/30 split. Hourly data from the bike
sharing dataset typically shows autocorrelation: neighbouring hours are similar in
weather, season and user behaviour. A random split therefore allows hours that are
close in time to land in both the training and the test set, which can overstate the
measured generalisation.

As a robustness check a temporal split was run: the first 70 percent of the data in
chronological order (2011-01-01 to 2012-05-27) served as the training set,
the remaining 30 percent (2012-05-27 to 2012-12-31) as the test set. The
results are worse compared to the random split (dRMSE XGBoost:
+39.64, EBM: +24.38; dR2 XGBoost: -0.0844,
EBM: -0.0357). The metric divergence is moderate, which suggests that
temporal leakage noticeably affects the random split performance.
For the XAI demonstration of this work the absolute model quality is secondary; all
pipelines use the same models and the same split, so the relative ranking of the
explanation pipelines is unaffected by this limitation.
